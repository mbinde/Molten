## Description (4000 chars max)
```
Molten is the complete inventory management app for glass artists. Whether you work with hot glass, torchwork, or kiln fusing, Molten helps you track your materials, plan shopping trips, and find suppliers—all from your iPhone or iPad.

COMPREHENSIVE CATALOG
Browse over 3,000 glass colors and coatings from more than 20 manufacturers including Bullseye, Effetre, Double Helix, Glass Alchemy, Northstar, Creation is Messy, and more. Full COE coverage from 33 to 104.

• Detailed product information for every item
• Add your own photos and personal notes
• Tag items for easy organization
• Advanced search by color, manufacturer, COE, and more

SMART INVENTORY TRACKING
Know exactly what you have and where it's stored.

• Track rods, frit, sheet, tubes, stringers, and more
• Record precise quantities with appropriate units
• Organize by storage location
• See your entire collection at a glance

LABEL PRINTING WITH QR CODES
Organize your studio with professional printed labels.

• Choose from 50+ label templates for any label sheet
• Fully customizable layouts and content
• Include QR codes that open directly in the app
• Scan to instantly view glass details or update inventory

INTELLIGENT SHOPPING LISTS
Never forget what you need to restock.

• Organize by your favorite suppliers
• Specify quantities needed
• Quick reference while shopping
• Transfer purchased items directly to inventory

SHARE WITH FRIENDS
Let others see what's in your collection.

• Share your inventory with friends and collaborators
• Perfect for coordinating trades or sales
• Avoid bringing duplicates to workshops
• Control who can see your inventory and revoke access anytime

FIND GLASS SUPPLIERS & SCHOOLS
Discover where to buy glass and learn new techniques.

• Browse suppliers near you or search by location
• Find educational institutions offering glass programs
• Store details and contact information

SYNC & BACKUP
Keep your data safe and accessible.

• iCloud sync across all your devices
• Optional versioned cloud backups with Pro subscription

HIGHLY CONFIGURABLE
Make Molten work the way you do.

• Reorganize the UI and change defaults to match how you work
• Customize units, display options, and organization
• Configure storage locations to match your studio
• Personalize the catalog with your own notes and photos

PRIVACY FIRST
Your creative work stays private. All data is stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud account unless you explicitly choose to share it with someone. We don't track you, sell your data, or share it with third parties.

---

Molten Pro unlocks cloud backups and inventory sharing. Subscribe monthly or yearly, or continue using the free version with full catalog, inventory, and shopping list features.

Questions or feedback? Contact us at info@moltenglass.app
```
